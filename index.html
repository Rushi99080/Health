            How to progress

            1. Pick a weight you can lift for the reps with 1–2 reps left in the tank.
            2. When you hit the top of the rep range for all sets, add 2.5 kg next time.
            3. Lift heavy even while losing fat. It keeps your muscle so the weight lost is fat.
            4. Warm up 5 minutes before, stretch 5 minutes after.
            5. Pain in joints means stop that exercise and ask a trainer.

            The truth about belly fat

            You cannot burn fat from one spot. Crunches make the ab muscles stronger, but the fat on top only goes when your whole body loses fat.

            What removes belly fat: a calorie deficit, lifting weights 4–5 days a week, 8,000+ steps, 7–8 hours of sleep, and less sugar and alcohol. Belly fat is usually the first to show gains and the last to leave, so keep going.

            Track your waist every Monday. If the waist number drops, belly fat is dropping.
