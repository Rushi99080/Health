# Cut to 80

A fat-loss app built around the 30-day home challenge: 92 kg → 80 kg, beginner workouts at home, about 2,100 kcal and 140–150 g protein a day.

**Open it:** https://rushi99080.github.io/Health/cut-to-80/

## What's inside

- **Today** – trend weight, a ruler from start to goal, the challenge day, and the 6-point daily checklist (workout, steps, protein, calories, water, sleep). Ticks fill in from what you log; you can also tick by hand.
- **Food** – search 120 Indian foods, favourites, "repeat yesterday", custom foods, and a 7-day meal plan scaled to your calories and protein with a weekly grocery list.
- **Workout** – the 30-day calendar (Strength A/B/C, cardio, recovery, rest, light day 29, progress check day 30). 2 sets in weeks 1–2, 3 sets in weeks 3–4, cardio time grows each week. Rules, equipment and safety tips are on the same screen. A gym Push/Pull/Legs program is there too for later.
- **Progress** – weight chart with a 7-day trend and planned path, predicted goal date, challenge summary, weekly report, waist and body measurements, progress photos, badges and full history.
- **Coach** – automatic alerts (plateau, losing too fast, low protein, weekend overeating, missed workout, low water, short sleep), your targets explained, and reminder times.

## Install on iPhone

Open the link in **Safari** → tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**. It then opens full screen like an app and works offline.

## Your data

- Everything is saved **only in this phone's browser**. Nothing goes to GitHub.
- Open **Profile** (person icon) → **Backup (JSON)** once a week and keep the file somewhere safe. **Import backup** brings it back on a new phone.
- Never upload a backup file to this repository — it is public.

## Good to know

- AI meal logging and the AI coach chat work only in the Claude version of this app. This GitHub version has everything else.
- Calories and protein are estimates for normal home portions.
- This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Stop a workout if you feel severe pain, dizziness or chest pain, and check with a doctor before starting if you have a health condition.
